## Style rules (Magnus)
- Medieval woodblock print: black ink linework and carved hatching on parchment, flat colour, orange as the only spot colour. Props and world pieces must match the backgrounds (ink on parchment, objects mostly paper-coloured); characters use bolder carved shapes. Never modern, painted, or glossy.
- Fire effects are yellow/orange woodblock flames.
- Black-knight enemy copies: solid, not ghostly. Only the cloth goes black with orange accents.
- Always fix spelling and grammar in Magnus's text, but keep his wording.
- Only Magnus's own words count as approval. Never overwrite old version folders; each change gets a new vN.
