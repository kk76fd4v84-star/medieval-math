# Magnus's card changes (from card_codex edits, 2026-11-09)
Source: /workspace/card_codex/edits_2026-11-09.json. "Upgrade" means picking the same card again (each pick = +1 level).

1. **Cleave** (knight): no flat extra target. Knight's strike hits the first enemy for 100% and the second for 50%. Each upgrade raises the second hit to 65%, 75%, 85%, 95%. The next upgrade after that also hits a third enemy. Cost: the crit band shrinks a little (harder to crit).
2. **Swing and a Miss** (knight): raises the knight's damage, and each upgrade raises it more. Cost: a wrong answer makes the knight's swing hit the party member behind him for 1/4 of their life. This happens once per run; upgrades don't add to it.
3. **Embers** (firestarter): raises fire damage. Cost: on a miss there is a 5% chance of friendly fire, plus 5% per upgrade.
4. **Shield Wall** (any): 50% chance to block the enemy's first attack in each fight. Each of up to 3 upgrades raises it, ending at 80%. Cost: the block band shrinks a little, which buying armour wins back.
5. **Storm Surge** is renamed **Thunder Fist** (dark wizard): a right answer has a 25% chance to stun one enemy. Upgrades stun 2 enemies, then 3. Cost: wizard sums +1 level harder. The +3 damage is removed.
6. **Overcharge** is renamed **High Voltage** (dark wizard): a right answer adds +3 lightning damage to every enemy (down from +6). Cost: a miss shocks one ally for 1 heart unit, never the last one.
7. **Rallying Cry**, new (knight): 5% chance the party strikes again. Cost: 5% chance the enemies strike as soon as the meter hits zero, without waiting.
8. **Weighted Axe**, new (executioner): +20% damage against enemies below 25% health. Upgrades raise it up to +100%, on top of his normal damage growth. Cost: a miss sticks the axe in the ground, and he skips his next turn.

Deleted: the two blank new cards. All meter push-back values are 0, so there is no push-back.
New cards need card art (Maggi), and Thunder Fist and High Voltage need their names and text updated.
